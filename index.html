How to read this dashboard
CWD (Max) — Category Weighted Distribution
Circana's distribution measure that weights each store by its sales in the category (here, spirits) rather than counting doors equally. It reads how available the product is to the shoppers driving the most category volume in a market. "(Max)" is the peak weekly CWD in the period.
Dollar Sales per Point of CWD
Total dollar sales divided by the CWD %. A velocity measure: how much a brand or SKU sells relative to the availability it has. Higher = it sells harder wherever it's carried (e.g. $506.1K ÷ 2.26 ≈ $224K per point).
Dollar Trend vs LY
% change in L52W dollar sales versus the prior year-ago 52 weeks (Circana "% change vs. year ago"). New items ramping off a small base show very large percentages.
Rank (Dollar)
The brand's (or SKU's) position when every brand (or SKU) in the read is ordered by L52W dollar sales, highest first. Brand and SKU ranks are separate universes.
